When cracks appear, do we mend or reveal?
Kintsugi teaches us to revere the broken,
like the serenity of a moonlit night,
a reminder to see beauty in the imperfect.
In embracing our flaws, we surrender to growth,
allowing the light of imperfection to illuminate our path.

This idea lives deeper in my book "The Nine Stitches".
