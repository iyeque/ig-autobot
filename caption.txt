Thanksgiving Hangover

Your heart can't actually burst from gratitude—that'd defeat the whole point—but sometimes I wonder if mine's doing push-ups trying.

Tiny actions compound like interest on your soul. A morning stretch becomes standing up straighter all day. One genuine compliment ripples outward until strangers feel compelled to tell you something nice.

The trick? Consistency looks less like motivation and more like showing up even when you don't feel particularly grateful or gracious.

Start small. Notice one thing today that exists solely to make existence marginally less terrible than it could be—the coffee temperature, the street musician's timing, how gravity keeps everything where you left it.

Let's see what chaos theory thinks about cascading kindness. Spoiler: it's probably better at math than empathy.
