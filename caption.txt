I tried to write a book on success by failing on purpose. Spoiler: I nailed both.

There’s a deliciously absurd thrill in slamming your ego into the wall of competence, only to find a hidden door behind the “Try Harder” sign. My catastrophes taught me more than any textbook ever could—maybe I’m wise, maybe I’m just an expert at productive screw‑ups.

Grab The Nine Stitches if you’re curious about what happens when you stop fleeing failure and start interrogating it over coffee. Sometimes the point isn’t winning; it’s savoring why losing feels oddly satisfying.
