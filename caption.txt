Part 2 — Forest Fires — Destruction as renewal

Embers of disruption ignite the path to renewal.
Like serotinous cones that crack open only in flames,
our resistance can also be the catalyst for growth
– a paradox that sets us free from what's holding us back.
In the ashes of what we thought was lost, we discover
the alchemical spark that fuels our transformation.
