Embracing the Unyielding
Like trees that bend and twist, we can learn to flex our boundaries, embracing the unpredictable – just as the wind carves serotinous cones, freeing seeds to grow anew. Resilience is not about being unbreakable, but about being whole in the cracks.
Take a lesson from the forest floor: imperfections are not weaknesses, but strengths that allow us to grow, to adapt, to thrive in the face of adversity.
