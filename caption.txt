Part 7 — Tides — Rhythms of surrender

When the current pulls too strong, do you fight or yield?
Like the river's curves, our lives adapt to the currents that shape us.
Water wears down the stones, yet carves paths that lead to new vistas.
In surrender, we find the elegance of flaws.
Take a breath, and let the force of life sweep you along.
