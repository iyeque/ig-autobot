Stop trying to fix everything.

The most interesting people aren't perfect—they're interesting because they're not. Your cracks are where the light gets in. Where did I read that? Oh right, everywhere, including this book I wrote while accidentally burning dinner three nights a week.

Embrace your kintsugi moment. Those gold repairs don't diminish the vase; they complete it.

Now excuse me while I go practice what I preach by failing spectacularly at something else.

I am curious what this brings up for you.
