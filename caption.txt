Part 8 — Serotinous Pines — Seeds that need fire

What if your greatest strength lies in embracing the uncertainty?
Like the serotinous cone, waiting for the perfect spark to ignite its growth, we too must learn to trust the timing of our own transformation. Its seeds, locked in a tight bud, require the heat of fire to burst forth into life.
Take a cue from nature and allow yourself to be patient, even in the midst of chaos – for it's often in stillness that we find the catalyst for our greatest growth.
