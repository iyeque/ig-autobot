Your metaphor game is strong but also problematic—I've literally never seen a caterpillar struggle through hardship to become a butterfly. They just... do their thing inside a cocoon while turning into soup before reassembling as wings. Very undignified process.

But here's what I learned anyway:

That metamorphosis isn't actually about suffering—that's us projecting trauma onto insects. The caterpillar doesn't feel bad about dissolving itself; it's just doing what it does. Maybe our transformations don't need to hurt so much either?

The real lesson: Sometimes growth looks like complete destruction followed by weird goop becoming something else. This applies beautifully to failed projects, breakups, career pivots—the things where your old version dissolves completely.

Don't romanticize the pain. Just trust that the chaos has instructions built-in.
