When imperfection shines, beauty unfurls.
In wabi-sabi, we find the elegance of impermanence,
the gentle cracks on a well-worn stone.
Like a serotinous cone releasing seeds, we can let go of our need for perfection and find growth in our imperfections.
Take away the masks we've created, and reveal the beauty of our unique flaws.
