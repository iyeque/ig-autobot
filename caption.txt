Just as nature adapts to change, how can we learn to be more resilient in the face of uncertainty?
Our first instinct isn’t grace; it’s usually panic. But what if our glitch was actually a feature update? Maybe resilience looks less like standing firm against the storm and more like learning which way the wind naturally wants you to bend. It turns out ‘adaptability’ is just a fancy word for being good at getting up after falling down. A lot. So maybe stop trying so hard not to break. Try breaking beautifully instead.
