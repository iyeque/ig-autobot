Choosing the hard road never feels good—spoiler: it won’t. The easy lane just loops you back to square one. Strength is forged in the exact seconds you want to quit and decide not to.

So embrace the pressure, not because suffering is noble, but because resistance actually builds you. Think of coral: it needs friction to calcify into something worth admiring.
