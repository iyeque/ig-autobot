What lies beneath the surface can set you free.
Our vulnerabilities hold the key to growth, much like the delicate pattern of a serotinous cone cracks open to reveal its seeds.
In the stillness of calm, our true nature awakens, whispering secrets to the infinite.
This paradox of strength and weakness is the elegance of flaws.
So, let go of the need to be perfect and instead, allow your imperfections to illuminate your path.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
