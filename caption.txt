Part 4 — Keystone Species

One delicate thread hangs by a single strand.
Like the keystone species that anchors a fragile ecosystem,
a small but vital element can hold an entire system together.
A single serotinous cone can spark a forest fire, igniting growth.
In nature, the intricate balance of life relies on these hidden supports.
So too in our lives, a small but crucial element can be the difference
between collapse and harmony – don’t underestimate the power of the small.

Someone in your circle needs this today.
