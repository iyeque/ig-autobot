Part 2 — The illusion of control

Letting go of the steering wheel.
In the microcosm of our lives, we try to control every curve,
but the road of becoming is paved with uncertainty.
Like the serotinous cones that require fire to release their seeds,
our growth often demands the burning away of old patterns.
So we must learn to surrender, to let the winds of change
guide us, and trust in the elegance of our flaws,
for it's in the spaces we can't control that we discover our true potential.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
