What happens when you try to move forward, yet remain rooted in the same spot?
Like a spiral staircase that appears to ascend, yet remains fixed at its base, our intentions can propel us forward, while our outcomes remain tied to our current circumstances.
This tension between intention and outcome is where we find ourselves, suspended between the desire to progress and the reality of our present.
