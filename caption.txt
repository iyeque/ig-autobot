Can you find peace in the midst of chaos?
Like a serotinous cone, holding onto life until the perfect moment,
we must release our thoughts to allow our inner strength to unfold.
As we learn to quiet the mind, we discover the resilience to face adversity.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
