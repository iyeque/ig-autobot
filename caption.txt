Ever notice how a single, almost invisible twitch can set the whole world wobbling? The Nine Stitches argues that the moments we brush off—avoiding eye contact, silencing a notification—are the real architects of our lives. While self‑help gurus shout about grand overhauls, the true revolution is in the microscopic failures we let slip by. Small actions beget patterns, habits, identities, and eventually, destinies.

Because who needs a life‑coach when your own neglect is the best teacher?
