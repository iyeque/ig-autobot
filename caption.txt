I failed upward so hard I invented a new gravitational constant.

Embracing uncertainty isn't about giving up—the ironies pile up faster than laundry—but recognizing that your meticulously crafted five-year plan was always just fancy fortune cookie wisdom anyway.

When you stop white-knuckling every outcome, weird things happen: doors open that weren't there before, usually attached to opportunities wearing questionable footwear.

Stop pretending you can control everything. Start being curious about what emerges when you don't.

This idea lives deeper in my book "The Nine Stitches".
