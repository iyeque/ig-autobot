Finding the silence isn't about shutting up—the universe already knows what you sound like. It's about stopping long enough to notice that your anxiety has a specific humming frequency.

But here's the thing: silence isn't empty. It's full of everything you weren't paying attention to while you were so busy being interesting.

So go ahead. Fail spectacularly. Then listen to how quiet success sounds when nobody's watching.
