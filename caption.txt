What if your thoughts were the threads that weave success?
Like a spider's web, our minds spin boundaries –
Intimacy and perception shape our flesh-boundary
where physical meets metaphysical.
To find power in your thoughts, learn to be still within,
for it's in the calm that our true potential begins to unfurl.
