When the world whispers, do you listen?
In the intricate dance of our senses,
lies a world of beauty, hidden in plain sight,
where fleeting moments become timeless.
Notice the delicate balance within,
and you'll find that the chaos you feared
is but a microcosm of the harmony you seek.

This idea lives deeper in my book "The Nine Stitches".
