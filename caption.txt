Your biggest failures might actually be your best teachers.


That moment when everything falls apart? Yeah, that's often where the real work begins. Instead of fighting against adversity, what if you treated obstacles as the universe's weirdest mentors?

Growth isn't linear. It's messy, uncomfortable, and usually involves crying in public restrooms while questioning all your life choices. But somewhere in that chaos, you find out who you really are—and trust me, spoiler alert: you'll survive it.

Embrace the stitches. They're holding something beautiful together.
