Part 6 — Why intention rarely matches outcome

When failure blooms, beauty unfurls.
Unintended consequences of our actions
like serotinous cones bursting into flame,
releasing seeds to the wind, reminding us
that growth often arises from the unexpected.

In everyday life, this means embracing the mess,
learning to find beauty in the imperfect, and
allowing ourselves to be shaped by the unexpected.
