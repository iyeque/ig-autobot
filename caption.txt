My ceramic mug cracked today and I felt something about it.

Turns out there's a Japanese concept called kintsugi where they repair broken pottery with gold, making the cracks the most beautiful part. Like your heart after it gets wrecked. Or this coffee mug after it survives my clumsiness.

Imperfection isn't the enemy; permanence is. Everything breaks eventually. Your job is deciding whether to throw away the beautiful mess or make it glitter.

I am curious what this brings up for you.
