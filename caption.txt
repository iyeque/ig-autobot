Embracing uncertainty is like trusting a GPS while driving through fog—the route might change, but you'll probably get somewhere interesting anyway.

Life requires this weird dance between having plans and being completely open to them falling apart beautifully.

Uncertainty isn't the enemy; rigid certainty usually is.

Let yourself not know everything. It's free.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
