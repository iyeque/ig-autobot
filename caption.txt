A broken mirror reveals a fragmented self.
Like kintsugi, beauty lies in the cracks, a map of the journey,
imperfections etched on the surface of our skin.
Can we learn to see failure as a work of art, a reflection of our strength, not our weakness?

Keep this close for the days you need it.
