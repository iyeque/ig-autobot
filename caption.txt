# In the midst of imperfection, can we find harmony?

Yes, but not how you think.

Most people want their cracks sealed perfectly—but what if the crack was always meant to glow? Like kintsugi, those Japanese repairs where broken pottery gets filled with gold until the damage becomes more beautiful than the original piece ever was.

Flaw → repair → something better.

It sounds fake. I know. But here's the thing: your scarred-up heart has probably been through enough that it could qualify as museum-worthy ceramics at this point. And if you don't believe me, remember that bioluminescent plankton only light up when they get stressed.

Stress makes us shine.

So maybe being imperfect isn't the problem. Maybe trying too hard to fix every flaw is actually keeping you from glowing.

Leave yourself cracked.

What part of this speaks to you.
