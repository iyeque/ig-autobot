In the worn lines of aged wood, a story unfolds.
Wabi-sabi teaches us to cherish the imperfections of life,
to find beauty in the weathered, the worn.
In the cracks of a tree, a map of seasons is etched.
May we learn to see the beauty in our own imperfections,
the lines that tell the story of our journey.
