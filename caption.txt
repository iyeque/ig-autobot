The whisper of a loved one's voice in your ear? Or perhaps it's the subtle hum of the universe, a vibration that echoes through every cell of your being? As I explore this liminal space in 'The Nine Stitches', I invite you to join me on a journey of discovery, to unravel the mysteries of where flesh meets the infinite. #TheNineStitches #Flesh #Infinite"
