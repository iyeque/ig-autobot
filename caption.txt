Ripples don't ask permission before spreading. Neither do your moods when your neighbor shows up unannounced at work, radiating their chaos into your carefully organized morning routine. That subtle tension in your shoulders? It's already traveling through the office air conditioning vent, landing in cubicles three floors down where someone else's productivity takes an unexpected vacation.

Awareness hack: Your bad day doesn't arrive alone. It brings friends—the coffee shop barista whose tip you forgot, the dog walker forced to wait while you sort out your emotional baggage in the crosswalk, the stranger behind you in line now running fifteen minutes late themselves. Emotions are contagious not because people want them to spread, but because they literally do whether anyone consents.

Become the person who notices when they've become weather.
