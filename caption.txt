Where does our brokenness become our beauty?
In kintsugi, we find that cracks are not weaknesses but
the scars that reveal our story, our history.
Like the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery,
we can find beauty in our imperfections.
Can we learn to see our own scars as a testament
to our resilience, a reminder that we've been through
something and come out the other side, stronger?
