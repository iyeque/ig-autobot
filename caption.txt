"The Leaning Tower of Pisa stands as a testament to the elegance of flaws. Its tilt, a result of the dynamic soil-structure interaction, has allowed it to survive earthquakes that would have toppled a more rigid structure. Chapter 3 of The Nine Stitches explores the beauty of imperfection and how it can become a source of resilience. Just like the Tower's tilt, our own imperfections can give us flexibility and adaptability in the face of adversity. 

But the question remains: what are the imperfections that have given you flexibility, that have allowed you to lean into life's challenges and emerge stronger? Share with us in the comments below how your own 'tilts' have helped you become more resilient. #TheNineStitches #Imperfection #Resilience"
