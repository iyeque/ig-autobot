I accidentally spilled coffee all over this question which feels metaphorically appropriate.

Finding beauty in your flaws isn't about romanticizing damage—it's realizing that perfection was never the point anyway. Your cracks aren't just where light gets in; they're proof you survived something.

The Japanese call it kintsugi: repairing pottery with gold so the breakage becomes more valuable than the original piece. We don't do that enough with ourselves.

Embrace being unpolished. That roughness means you've actually been living things instead of watching them happen from behind glass.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
