Stop treating brokenness like a badge. It's the easiest way to avoid effort.

We adore cracked walls, peeling paint, rusted metal-as long as they stay exactly that way. A cracked vase becomes "vintage"; a fresh coat of paint becomes "work."

Kintsugi isn't about the shatter; it's about what you do with the fragments after the break.

Function over fashion. A reliable car beats a rust-caked show...
