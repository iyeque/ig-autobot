Will you surrender to the unknown, or let fear hold you back?
Vulnerability is the canvas where our true selves are painted
Each brushstroke a risk, yet a chance to discover
The elegance of flaws, where growth and imperfection entwine.
In the stillness, we find the courage to begin again,
To unravel the threads of what we think we know.

Someone in your circle needs this today.
