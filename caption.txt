"Where Flesh meets the Infinite

As we navigate the intricate web of perception, we often find ourselves entangled in the threads of cognitive bias. These systematic errors in thinking distort our intention vs. outcome, blinding us to the reality of our actions. 

In Chapter 1 of The Nine Stitches, we touched on the Dunning-Kruger effect, where individuals overestimate their abilities, leading to a disconnect between intention and outcome. Meanwhile, the self-serving bias creeps in, causing us to attribute our successes to our own brilliance and our failures to external circumstances.

But what about the biases that shape our view? Do we lean towards the Dunning-Kruger, convinced of our own invincibility, or do we succumb to self-serving bias, deflecting blame? Which bias do you think distorts your perception the most?
