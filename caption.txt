"Where does our digital extension end? As we weave technology into the fabric of our lives, our bodies become the interface between the physical and metaphysical. Just as our skin marks the boundary where the physical meets the metaphysical, our devices and implants blur the lines between flesh and circuitry. We've come a long way from the first spectacles that extended our vision, to the neural interfaces that read our thoughts. But with each advancement, we're forced to ask: what's the limit of our digital reach? Do our wearable devices and implantable chips expand our senses, or do they redefine what it means to be human? To truly integrate our digital selves, we must first understand the boundaries of our flesh. #TheNineStitches #Technology #Extension"
