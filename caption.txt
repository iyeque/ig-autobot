Part 10 — Ecosystems — Everything affects everything

When we try to hold still, the world keeps moving.
Our presence is a ripple that touches all other ripples in the pond.
Like kintsugi, our flaws make us whole and beautiful.
We are part of the intricate web of life, connected to everything.
So be present, be imperfect, and let your uniqueness shine.
