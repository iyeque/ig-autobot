What if resistance only fuels the fire, while surrender ignites the spark?
Adversity is like a stormy sea, yet within its waves lies an opportunity for growth,
just as the phoenix rises from the ashes, reborn and radiant.
Take the lesson of the ear thworm: it burrows through the darkness, unafraid to be broken,
for in its fragments lies the beauty of its unique terrain.
