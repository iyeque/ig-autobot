Trying not to succeed at failing is basically professional procrastination.

Life’s weirdest trick? When you decide your goal is to not mess up, you've already messed up. It's like trying to hold water in cupped hands while standing under a waterfall—the moment you focus on keeping dry, you're definitely getting soaked.

The deeper you look into yourself, the less you see. Like those Russian dolls, except instead of smaller dolls inside, there's just more questions asking where all the answers went. Sometimes you gotta trust the chaos has a rhythm even when you can't hear it.

The best failures teach us nothing—that's their secret.
