My bones are made of dust and stubbornness.

That quote hits different when you've actually been broken—not metaphorically, I mean literally. Like, there was a thing that happened, and suddenly you understand why people say pain changes you. It doesn't make you stronger; it just makes you better acquainted with gravity.

But here's what I learned while picking myself off various floors: resilience isn't some heroic quality you're born with. It's more like... muscle memory for disappointment. Every time you get back up (even if nobody's watching), even if you're shaking, even if you don't believe the bullshit motivational posters—the neurons fire together and eventually wire themselves into something resembling courage.

So maybe stop trying to be resilient and instead practice returning to standing position after falls. That's all growth ever really is.
