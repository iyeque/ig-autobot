I don't know what I'm doing most of the time, which honestly makes everything more interesting.

Wabi-sabi isn't about forcing imperfection—it's noticing what was always there once you stop photoshopping reality. That crack in your phone screen? It's been having a great time traveling across the surface.

Cultivate imperfection:
Stop comparing your first draft to someone else's final product
Keep one obviously flawed version of anything important to you
Ask yourself "Is this interestingly broken or just poorly made?"

The Japanese didn't invent tea bowls with asymmetric handles so people would focus on flaws—they noticed existing asymmetry and thought "oh, that's beautiful."
