I spent years building elaborate systems to avoid failure. Turned out I was just engineering more complicated ways to trip over myself.

The thing about letting go isn't some zen moment where everything becomes clear—the universe doesn't suddenly hand you a manual because you stopped clenching your fists so hard. Instead, you notice all the ridiculous things you were gripping onto in the first place.

Freedom in uncertainty feels less like flying and more like realizing gravity still applies, but now you're not fighting it every second. You stumble into something resembling peace not despite the chaos, but because you stop treating chaos like a personal insult.

Here's what nobody tells you: uncertainty isn't empty space waiting to fill you up with purpose. It's just... there. And sometimes "there" includes you failing at basic adult tasks while simultaneously having profound thoughts about existence. Both can be true.

Grab The Nine Stitches wherever books exist—or don't! Either way, probably worth figuring out why you keep doing the same thing expecting different results. 😉...
