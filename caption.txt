When steps harmonize yet diverge, what do we find?
Intention and outcome, like two dancers entwined,
their paths diverging with each subtle move.
In this paradox, we find the beauty of impermanence.
Let your steps be guided by the elegance of flaws,
for it's in the imperfections that we find our true harmony.
