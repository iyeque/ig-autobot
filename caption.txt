Oh great, another chance to excel at failure. Ever notice how breakthroughs feel suspiciously like giving up? My “unavoidable challenge” is accepting I’ll never master the art of folding a fitted sheet. It sits there, a wrinkled monument to my spatial deficiencies, taunting me. Yet every botched attempt teaches humility (and the true meaning of “good enough”). So here’s to glorifying our clumsy defeats. What’s yours?
