"When we intentionally try to succeed, we often create a paradox. Our goals become the very thing that stands in the way of our desired outcome. It's like trying to grasp a mist - the more we try to hold on, the more it slips through our fingers. 

Consider the way a tree grows, with its roots digging deep into the earth, yet its branches reaching for the sky. It doesn't try to force its way up, but instead, it adapts to the terrain, embracing the wind, the sun, and the rain. What happens when our goals and outcomes diverge? Do we become like the tree, or do we try to force the issue? #TheNineStitches #WabiSabi #ParadoxOfProductiveFailure"
