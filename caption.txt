In the imperfections of our flesh, beauty begins.
Like the intricate patterns on an earthen skin, flaws tell the story of a life lived. The elegance of impermanence, a wabi-sabi beauty.
Embracing our own 'flesh meets the infinite' moments, we find the beauty in the broken, the worn, and the worn-out.
