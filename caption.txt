Let me tell you what I learned from watching plants die on my windowsill while I pretended to water them correctly:

Potential isn't some magical thing hiding inside you like treasure in your belly button lint. It's more like... bacteria. Every living system has this baseline capacity to do something when conditions align.

But here's where we go wrong: We treat our "potential" like a single oak tree that needs perfect soil, sunlight, and weekly affirmations. Meanwhile, we're actually more like those resilient weeds cracking through concrete—the kind that survives car exhaust and people kicking them.

Stop asking "what am I capable of?" and ask instead: "Under these exact weird circumstances, with this specific mess I'm dealing with right now, what tiny growth pattern could happen?"

Because becoming doesn't require you to bloom into something unrecognizable. Sometimes it just means surviving winter differently than expected.
