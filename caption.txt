Trying to fix one tiny thing often ends up breaking everything else.
A butterfly effect, but clumsier. Like spilling coffee while trying not to spill it.
That single act can shift your entire morning from ‘calm professional’ to ‘chaos muppet’. But what if that spilled coffee forces a conversation you otherwise wouldn't have had? A connection made possible because of the mistake. Your attempt to control the outcome created the exact conditions needed for growth.
Turns out, the universe doesn’t reward perfect plans; it rewards beautiful accidents.

If this moved you, you will find more in "The Nine Stitches".
