Part 9 — Becoming Through Breaking

When we shatter, we become something new.
Like a piece of pottery broken and restored, our flaws are what make us whole – a testament to the elegance of imperfection. Kintsugi reminds us that it's in the breaking that we find beauty.
So, let go of the need for perfection and allow yourself to be imperfect, for it's in those cracks that growth and transformation take hold.
