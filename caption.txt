Let's stop romanticizing this whole burning-down thing.

Yes, yes, rising from ashes sounds poetic when you've never actually been cremated. But here's what they don't tell you in those motivational memes: most things that burn simply turn into ash and stay ash. There is no guarantee of resurrection. Sometimes there's just... smoke damage.

That said, I'm not saying don't fail spectacularly. Fail all you want! Just maybe keep some water nearby. And remember: even a phoenix needs a new nest after every fire. Rebirth isn't free real estate—you still gotta do all the work of building something new.

Fail forward? More like fail sideways into another dimension where your mistakes follow you like hungry ghosts.

But hey, at least you'll have stories.
