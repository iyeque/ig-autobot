Sure, chaos theory looks gorgeous on a blackboard when you're describing weather systems, but try explaining to anyone at your office that not replying to an email was actually a profound act of rebellion against the tyranny of digital communication. They'll look at you funny because they understand something you don't-that you didn't change anything except their perception of whether you were competent.

The real magic isn't in any one person's choice. It's in the aggregate. You know those people who insist they'd have voted differently in some historical election? Utterly meaningless.
