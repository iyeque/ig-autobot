Part 2 — The illusion of control

Nothing prepares you for what happens when you stop controlling everything.

I spent years building elaborate plans, contingency strategies, backup contingencies—and somehow still ended up exactly where I wasn't trying to go.

Maybe that's the point? The universe has better taste than our spreadsheets do.

Stop micromanaging your destiny.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
