Part 3 — Honeybees — Collective intelligence

What if your failures were a catalyst for growth?

Just as the hive shares a single intention,
our senses tune in to the world around us,
expanding our boundaries of perception.

Can we learn to see our mistakes as opportunities
for growth, rather than threats to our ego?
