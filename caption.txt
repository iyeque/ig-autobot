Picture this: You wake up with a mission. A glorious, noble intention burning at your core-let's call it "Achieving Work-Life Balance" because that cljerkic failure is always reliable. Your central star ignites with purpose. The first few orbits around your daily routine feel right. You meditate! You work for exactly eight hours! You have dinner with friends who don't talk about spreadsheets!
But here’s the thing about galaxies-they're lazy. They want equilibrium. And your beautiful intention? It's an anomaly. An unstable perturbation in the fabric of your established chaos.
So what does the universe do? It starts the spiral.
One day, something minor breaks sync. Maybe you get an urgent email during dinner. Not a catastrophe, just a small pull from the outer ring. But now there's tension between your centered intention ("I am present") and the orbiting reality (you're thinking about that reply). To resolve the dissonance, you compromise. You check the email quickly, telling yourself it doesn't count.
That tiny concession creates its own gravitational field. Next week, another emergency crops up. Then another. Each one feels justified individually but collectively they're dragging you off-axis. Your galaxy isn't spiraling anymore-it's wobbling.
