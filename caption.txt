Surrender isn’t a defeat; it’s the quiet admission that your grand plan was always a house of cards. It’s the moment after you’ve wrestled with IKEA instructions and finally accept that one screw will forever be the odd one out. Elegance arrives when you stop battling chaos and start admiring the beautiful randomness—like watching someone else’s epic parallel‑parking fail. Perhaps peace isn’t about controlling outcomes, but about savoring the spectacular mess along the way. Read more in “The Nine Stitches” by M.W.E. Wigman.
