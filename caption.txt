Tension: Intention vs. Outcome

The best-laid plans dissolve into beautiful disasters more often than not. But here's the thing: that's where growth lives—not in the pristine execution, but in the glorious mess.

Intention is your compass pointing north while outcome is the actual direction you ended up traveling. Sometimes they meet. Often, they don't. And somehow, that gap becomes the most interesting part of the journey.

Reality? It's less rigid than we'd like to admit. Uncertainty isn't our enemy pretending to be chaos—it's the texture of everything worth doing.

Tell me how this lands for you.
