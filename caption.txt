Uncertainty can feel like standing naked in front of your high school crush while they judge whether you've aged well.

But here's the thing: every certainty you cling to is probably wrong anyway. Your phone will update tomorrow. That career path you mapped out? Already obsolete. The person you think you need to impress has their own private disasters happening behind closed doors.

So maybe uncertainty isn't what you should fear. Maybe it's the most honest state there is—the acknowledgment that becoming means being constantly unfinished.

Embrace the not-knowing. It's where all interesting things live.

Someone in your circle needs this today.
