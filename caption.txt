Stop scrolling and look around for once.

Everything beautiful exists in the cracks—the coffee ring stain on your desk, the way light hits broken glass, the perfect asymmetry of a cracked vase held together with gold.

Paying attention isn't productivity hack; it's remembering how to see what was always there. Your brain rewards novelty so chase the weird details everyone else filters out.

Now go find something ugly and stare at it until it becomes interesting.
