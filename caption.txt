Want to know where magic actually hides?

Not in being flawless—but in those weird gaps between what you wanted and what happened. That crooked stitch, the burned edge, the typo that became your whole aesthetic.

Our cracks aren't where we break; they're where light gets interesting. The Japanese call this kintsugi—the art of repairing pottery with gold, making the damage more beautiful than the original.

Your flaws aren't obstacles to overcome. They're invitations to get creative. The universe already knows you're imperfect, so stop trying to hide it from yourself. Instead, ask better questions:

How can I make this weird thing I did into something intentional?
What would happen if I leaned INTO the mistake instead of away from it?

Because perfect people don't exist. Only people who've learned to dance while carrying their awkwardness differently.
