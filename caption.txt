I tried to write this post about how tiny choices create ripples I'll never see, but somehow ended up staring at a wall for three hours.

Turns out every action has consequences—but also, nobody cares as much as they pretend to. Your coffee choice won't save the planet, your career pivot won't topple capitalism, and that one mean comment online definitely didn't destroy someone's soul (probably).

But here's what I learned: Embracing imperfection isn't lazy optimism. It's recognizing that most things operate on scales far above our influence anyway. So pick something meaningful to you and do it badly until you get decent at it.

Because trying to control everything? That's actually just another kind of failing upward.
