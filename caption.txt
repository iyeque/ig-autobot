"Imagine a serene lake, with each ripple gently spreading outward, carrying the soothing energy of self-compassion. As we cultivate kindness within ourselves, it seeps into our relationships, creating a ripple effect of understanding and empathy. Just as the ripples from a stone tossed into the water intersect with the shore, our self-compassion intersects with the world, influencing those around us. But what happens when we're faced with adversity, and our own imperfections threaten to disrupt the calm waters of our self-compassion? Can we still choose to be calm, even when the world around us is in turmoil? #TheNineStitches #Mindfulness #Compassion"
