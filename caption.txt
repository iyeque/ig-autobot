I've got this theory that punctuality was invented by people who were secretly terrified of spontaneity.

Time isn't broken—we just treat it like a rigid spreadsheet when it's actually more of a... messy poetry collection. Late mornings aren't failures; they're negotiations with gravity.

Those Japanese found something called ma—the perfect amount of empty space between things—and somehow we forgot to apply it to our schedules. Why do we cram every second full when even trees drop leaves at random intervals?

Beauty lives in the spaces we don't fill.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
