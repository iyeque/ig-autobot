Let’s be honest. When someone throws out the comparison between life and a river, there’s an immediate, almost saccharine sense of relief. Like saying, "Well, at least it’s moving!" Because movement implies progress, doesn't it? It suggests purpose. We want to believe we are perpetually charting courses toward some glittering delta where success finally pools and allows us to dip a perfectly manicured hand into the cool water.

But look closer. Really look.

A river isn't always a benevolent artist. Sometimes, it’s a geological tyrant. It drills through solid granite with grinding, relentless indifference. It bypasses fertile meadows only to carve desolate canyons. Its passage is rarely tidy; it's usually messy, punctuated by flash floods, unexpected rapids, and those frustrating stretches where the current seems to sigh deeply right past your intended bend.

And we are that river.
