Tried building a pyramid out of Jenga blocks once. Failed spectacularly when I sneezed during construction.

But here's what I learned: most "failures" aren't single points—they're cascading systems where one thing failing makes everything else collapse into itself like bad origami. The key isn't avoiding failure; it's designing things so they fail gracefully instead of taking down the whole operation.

Watch how bamboo doesn't snap under pressure—it bends. Your projects should do that too.

This idea lives deeper in my book "The Nine Stitches".
