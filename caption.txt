Trees don't stress about being knocked down—they've got centuries to figure it out.

Their secret weapon? Staying rooted while still bending. Rigid things break; flexible things sway through hurricanes and come back stronger.

Maybe your version of resilience isn't standing perfectly straight. Maybe it's knowing which parts can bend, which branches you can afford to lose, and trusting your core will hold even when everything looks broken above ground.

Let yourself be imperfect wood.

This idea lives deeper in my book "The Nine Stitches".
