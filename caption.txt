Recognizing your place in the cosmic spiderweb means every choice ripples outward like a stone thrown into still water—or more accurately, like dropping acid into a bathtub full of Jell-O while everyone's trying to sleep.

Small decisions cascade into unintended consequences faster than you can say "I meant to do that." Suddenly your lazy afternoon becomes someone else's Monday morning nightmare.

The trick isn't avoiding connection; it's accepting that responsibility feels better than willful ignorance (eventually).
