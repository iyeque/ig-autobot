Between the spaces where we're not quite here or there.
The in-between is where our boundaries merge with the unknown,
where our senses adapt and transform, and where growth unfolds.
In the threshold, we discover the elegance of our own fragility.
So the next time you're stuck in transition, remember, it's in the
liminal spaces where the beauty of growth and transformation await.
