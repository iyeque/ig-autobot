Beyond the skin of what we know, lies what?

Our senses extend the limits of our flesh, touching the infinite. Yet, personal space varies, creating an intimate dance with the unknown. Over millennia, our perception has evolved, adapting to the ever-changing environment.

Perhaps the horizon of our expectations is not something to be reached, but a threshold to be transcended, allowing us to tap into the elegance of our own flaws.
