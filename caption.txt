I tried being grateful once. Turned into a Hallmark card.

But seriously—gratefulness isn't some cosmic lottery ticket. It's more like composting your emotional garbage so something actually useful grows from it.

Those micro-moments where you catch yourself noticing light through leaves or the fact someone made coffee add up faster than you'd think. Each small acknowledgment rewires your brain's default setting from "what's wrong" to "huh, interesting."

Start tiny. Notice one thing today. Then two tomorrow. Watch how quickly your operating system updates itself—noticing beauty becomes as automatic as checking your phone every four minutes.

Small stitches close big wounds.

This idea lives deeper in my book "The Nine Stitches".
