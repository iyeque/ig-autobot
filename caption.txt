Part 2 — Forest Fires — Destruction as renewal

Like a seed, I must be consumed by fire to sprout anew.
Serotinous cones bursting with potential,
releasing seeds only after a fiery ripeness. So too,
must we be willing to transform through adversity.
Don't be afraid to let your edges char,
for it's in the ashes that new growth begins.

Tell me how this lands for you.
