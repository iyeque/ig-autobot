I tried to write this entire chapter while eating cereal, and somehow I think it actually made the writing better?

The thing nobody tells you about failure: when you try to fail spectacularly, you accidentally stumble into some genuinely interesting work. It's like when you meant to order coffee but typed "coffe" instead and discovered your local cafe has a secret menu.

Trying to embrace failure doesn't mean succeeding less—it means noticing more. Those cracks where light gets in? Yeah, they were always there. We just needed permission from ourselves to look sideways.

Turns out the ripples don't care whether you intended them or not. Might as well enjoy watching them spread.

I am curious what this brings up for you.
