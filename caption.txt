Stop treating small actions like they don't matter—they do.

That presentation you bombed influenced your manager's quarterly review, which affected their confidence talking to THEIR boss, which changed company direction. You're not just a cog; you're a moss-covered rock shifting entire forests.

Be deliberately consequential. Your tiny ecosystem needs you as much as a coral reef needs its polyps. Even when you screw up spectacularly, track what happened next. That's where power hides.
