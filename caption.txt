What if our fragility was our greatest asset?
We are bound by skin, yet our senses extend far beyond,
like roots that dig deep into the earth, and branches that touch the sky.
The elegance of flaws lies in their intricate, interconnected design.
In our daily lives, we can find resilience in embracing our imperfections.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
