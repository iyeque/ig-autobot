Freedom isn’t the antithesis of constraint; it’s the ugly miracle that erupts when you shove against the walls until they crack. Every painter wrestles a canvas, every inventor battles physics, every writer fights a blank page—yet the breakthroughs bloom precisely because the cage exists. True creative liberty isn’t “do‑anything” chaos; it’s mastering the very chains that once felt like shackles. In other words, the cage becomes a studio, the friction a muse, and the limits… well, they’re just the universe’s way of keeping us humble.
