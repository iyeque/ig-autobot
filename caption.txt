When the waves of reality crash against our shores, can we ride the surrender?

In the stillness of a lake, we find the elegance of letting go –
the ripples of thought, the calm of acceptance
the surrender to the unknown.

In everyday life, this means embracing uncertainty,
finding peace in the midst of chaos –
and trusting that the universe has our back.

This idea lives deeper in my book "The Nine Stitches".
