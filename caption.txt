# 'If you try to fail, you must succeed'

Here's where things get deliciously contradictory:

Trying to fail means you've already succeeded—at trying.

The moment you set out with pure intention to crash and burn, you've automatically proven you care enough to show up. Congratulations! That's not failure; that's presence.

Most of us spend our lives accidentally succeeding at avoiding success through fear. But when you deliberately court disaster? You bypass the anxiety entirely.

It's the difference between: "I'm too scared to mess up" versus "Let me see what happens when I do."

Paradox solved: Trying proves you're invested. So go ahead—fail gloriously.
