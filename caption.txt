When the tiniest thread unravels the entire tapestry.
Like the serotinous cone releases seeds with a spark of fire, the intricate dance of microcosm and macrocosm holds secrets we can only grasp by embracing the smallest details. The patterns we find in the earthworm's tunnel system or the squid's iridescent skin whisper truths about our own place in the universe.
So let the intricate beauty of the smallest parts remind you that the elegance of flaws and imperfections is what makes us whole – and that to become, be calm, and to be calm, pretend to be calm.
