"The price of survival is often a sacrifice of what we hold dear. Like a lizard that sheds its tail to escape predators, have you ever had to 'autotomize' a part of yourself to survive? Perhaps it was a relationship, a dream, or a piece of your identity. Chapter 1 of The Nine Stitches explores the paradox of immediate survival vs. long-term costs. What have you had to leave behind to stay alive?
