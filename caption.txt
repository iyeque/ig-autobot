Part 2 — If You Can’t Evade It, Embrace It

What if the only way to truly thrive is to surrender to what's already here?
Embracing the inevitable, like a tree embracing the wind,
we find strength in the flexibility of our own boundaries.
The earthworm's skin adapts to the soil,
the squid's chitin to the ocean's darkness.
Your most powerful moments often arise from embracing what you cannot change.
