Our brains love a good plot twist—until reality decides to rewrite the script. Cue cognitive dissonance, that delightful existential spin‑cycle where you replay “what‑if” scenarios like a broken Spotify playlist on repeat.

The Nine Stitches taught me that failure isn’t always a bad forecast; sometimes it’s just a wildly unrealistic expectation—like insisting a cake will rise in five minutes because you’re starving. Physics doesn’t care how badly you want a soufflé to defy gravity.

The secret? Stop treating life like a chess match where every piece must be controlled. Think jazz improvisation: set your intentions, then let the universe toss in a few off‑beat notes. The magic happens in the gaps.
