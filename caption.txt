Just tried to organize one shelf of my kitchen and now I'm questioning whether individual agency even matters.

Here's the thing: your tiny decisions—the coffee brand you pick, the text you send left unsent, the song stuck in your head—are simultaneously meaningless and everything. Like how a single stitch doesn't make a quilt, but also literally does?

We think our impact needs to be visible from space, but what if beauty exists precisely because nothing is visible all at once? The macrocosm isn't watching us build it; we ARE building it, one seemingly insignificant choice at a time.

Maybe the point isn't to matter more. Maybe it's to participate better.

The micro is already contained within the macro.

This idea lives deeper in my book "The Nine Stitches".
