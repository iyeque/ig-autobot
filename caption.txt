Ah yes, the mind. That magnificent little engine that's been running since roughly 2 AM when you decided it was absolutely critical to replay every embarrassing moment of your life in high definition while everyone else sleeps peacefully.

Here's what I've learned about thought: most people treat their minds like hotel rooms they've checked into but never cleaned up after. There's takeout containers from decisions made years ago still sitting on the nightstand. Half-finished opinions about politics left on the bathroom counter. An entire wing dedicated to "what if" scenarios that would make a screenwriter blush at how dramatically overblown they are.

We're told constantly that our thoughts create reality. And there's truth to that - but let me offer a corrective. Thoughts don't just create external reality. They create your reality first. You walk through the same world as ten other people standing next to you, and each of you experiences eleven different universes because each mind edits the footage differently. Some of us pan across the beauty. Others zoom obsessively on the crack in the sidewalk and narrate it with apocalyptic tension music.
