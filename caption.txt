Where Flesh meets the Infinite.

The body, wrapped in flesh marks the boundary
where the physical meets the metaphysical.
In a physical sense, the body is flesh bound
by skin—a dynamic parchment upon which
the story of our senses is inscribed.

Can we find beauty in the imperfect, the broken, and the whole?
In embracing our flaws, we may just discover our true selves.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
