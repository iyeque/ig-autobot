"The boundaries of success are often defined by the world around us, but what happens when we try to fail and succeed on our own terms? In Chapter 1 of The Nine Stitches, I explore how narrow success standards can lead to mental health costs and a disconnection from our true selves.

Just like the limits of our physical bodies, the boundaries of our perception are shaped by societal expectations. We're conditioned to believe that success is a linear path, with each milestone leading to the next. But what if we're trying to succeed in a way that doesn't align with our values or passions?

Imagine a tree that's grown in a forest, its branches reaching for the sky. But what if its growth is stunted by the pressure to conform to a predetermined shape? Would it not be more beautiful, more resilient, if it were allowed to flourish in its own unique way?

So, I ask you: what does success look like when no one is watching? Is it a quiet moment of reflection, a burst of creativity, or a simple act of kindness? Perhaps it's a blend of all these things, a tapestry woven from the threads of our true selves.
