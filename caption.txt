You know how trees get all those ugly scarred patches? Turns out they don't heal like humans do—the wound stays forever.

That split from five years ago when your best friend ghosted you? It doesn't close up neatly back into pristine bark. Instead, new growth wraps around it, incorporating the damage until what was once trauma becomes structure.

This is why I'm weirdly obsessed with old trees. They're walking proof that survival isn't about avoiding damage—it's about making your broken parts useful again. Your deepest flaws often become your strongest support beams.

Also worth noting: these battle-worn trunks produce the most beautiful wood grain. Just saying.
