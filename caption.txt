Part 6 — Mycelium — Invisible networks

What happens when we hit the limits of our skin?
The senses we rely on, like threads in a tapestry, weave together our reality.
Personal space, a dynamic boundary, shifts with each encounter.
The boundaries we set, like threads in a spider's web, are both fragile and resilient.
Yet, in our daily lives, we often neglect the unseen networks that support us,
like the intricate web of relationships, the quiet strength of a loved one's presence.
