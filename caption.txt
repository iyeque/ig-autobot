Let's stop pretending we're not all secretly superheroes whose daily decisions create butterfly effects across dimensions.

Every choice sends out invisible ripples—but here's the twist: most of them die out before reaching shore. Not every decision reshapes reality; some just reshape your grocery list.

The real question isn't how to see consequences clearly. It's learning which impacts actually matter versus which ones just make great Instagram stories.

Be curious, not paralyzed. Try stuff, watch what happens, adjust. The universe has way less invested in your perfect outcome than you do.

Now go forth and accidentally save/destroy something small.

Keep this close for the days you need it.
