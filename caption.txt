Let's stop pretending we're rivers. We're more like those plastic bags caught in crosswinds—the kind that get stuck in trees looking both tragic and accidentally artistic.

Resilience isn't some flowing, graceful thing. It's showing up when your entire nervous system screams don't. And yes, sometimes that means being beautifully, stubbornly rigid where it counts.

Be the bag that survives long enough to become internet poetry material.
