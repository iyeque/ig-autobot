Ever seen a text that looks totally legit? Like, "Your package is on its way!" or "Thanks for using our service!" But then they ask for your login or pjerkword? That’s the red flag. Here are 3 sneaky ones I’ve spotted lately-share these with your kids so they know what to trash immediately. 🚮

1. The "Account Security" Alert: It says something like, "We detected unusual activity on your account. Please confirm your identity here:" followed by a sketchy link. Real companies will never ask you to log in via text.

2. The "Free Trial" Offer: This one promises a free trial for a game or streaming service if you just enter your username and pjerkword. Spoiler alert: it’s not free, and now they have your keys.

3. The "Too Good To Be True" Giveaway: "Congrats! You won a $100 gift card! Claim it now!" All you need to do is "log in" to see your prize. Don’t fall for it. Your kid’s gaming accounts aren’t worth losing over fake money.

Teach them this rule: Pjerkwords are private. Period. If anyone asks for it via text, block and delete.
