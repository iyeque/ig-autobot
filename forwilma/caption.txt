I know how exciting it can be to explore the online world, especially when something catches your eye - like an intriguing message or a cool link. But before you click, let's make sure we're staying safe together. Here are three quick checks you should do:

1️⃣ Check the Sender: Is this someone you trust? If you don’t recognize the sender, be cautious. Scammers often disguise themselves as friends or people you might know.

2️⃣ Look at the Link: Hover over the link without clicking. Does it look strange or unusual? Sometimes, links can hide where they’ll actually take you. Be wary if it doesn’t match what you expect.

3️⃣ Think Before You Click: Ask yourself, does this feel right? Trust your instincts. If something seems too good to be true or just feels off, it's better to be safe and skip it.

These simple steps can help protect you from online scams. Stay safe out there, and remember, a little caution goes a long way in keeping our digital journey fun and secure!
