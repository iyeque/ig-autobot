As parents, we often chase this elusive thing called "balance." We try to manage work deadlines, school pickups, homework help, and bedtimes-all while maintaining some semblance of our own identities outside of being moms and dads. I'm right there with you; as the founder of Digital Guardian and a parent myself, I juggle multiple responsibilities every day. But here's what I've come to realize: true "balance" might just be a myth.

Balance suggests an even distribution between all aspects of our lives. But life isn't static; it ebbs and flows. One week, work might demand more attention. The next, my 2-year-old daughter might have a fever and need constant care. What we're really after isn't balance, but flexibility-the ability to pivot when life throws us curveballs.

When my daughter was born two years ago, I thought I could maintain everything at once. I quickly learned that wasn't possible. My husband and I had to adapt, to lean on each other more heavily during certain times, and to prioritize what truly mattered most.
