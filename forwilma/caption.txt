For Bluesky:

I hear the term "work-life balance" and honestly? It feels like one more thing on my to-do list. Like another standard I’m failing to meet. Between building Digital Guardian, keeping our 2-year-old from coloring the walls with a diaper, and just… existing, the idea of a perfectly balanced day seems like a myth. A really pretty, unattainable myth.

Instead, I’m trying to reframe it. Not balance, but integration. Some days, work spills into dinner prep. Other days, a tantrum over a misplaced toy derails a whole morning of coding. The goal isn't to have perfect, separate compartments. The goal is to be present for the moments that matter-the messy, chaotic, beautiful ones. It's less about juggling and more about accepting that some balls will drop. And that’s okay. We're building this to help families navigate their own version of "integrated chaos," not to achieve some impossible ideal.

What part of your life feels most out of balance right now?
