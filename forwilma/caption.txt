The 2-minute notification tweak that changed my workday

As a busy mom and entrepreneur, I'm always looking for ways to boost productivity and sanity. One simple change to my phone notifications has made a huge difference - setting aside a dedicated 2-minute window each hour to check and respond.

Before making this switch, I was constantly interrupted by pings and beeps throughout the day. Every alert demanded my immediate attention, derailing focus and leaving me frazzled. My young daughter would often interrupt too, asking for snacks or attention at precisely the wrong moments. 

I realized I needed a better system to manage the constant stream of messages, emails, and app updates. So, I designated two minutes per hour as "Notification Time." During these brief intervals, I quickly scan through my feeds, tend to urgent matters, and plan my next tasks.

This tiny tweak has had a profound impact on my workflow. By batching notifications, I can stay connected without the distraction of constant interruptions. It also gives me more control over when and how I engage with my family. My daughter still requires care, but now she knows exactly when Mom will be available, which reduces her anxious requests.

In fact, studies show that frequent, short breaks can actually enhance concentration and creativity. Research from the University of Michigan found that workers who took regular breaks performed better than those who worked long stretches without interruption.[1] 

Give it a try! Set aside just 2 minutes every hour to tame your notifications and reclaim some sanity in your workday. You might be surprised at the boost it gives you.
